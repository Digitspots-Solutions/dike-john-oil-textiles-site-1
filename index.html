Welcome to DIKE JOHN OIL & TEXTILES

Situated at 2/4 Onyenso Street Off 45 Omenazu by Faulks Road, Aba North, we are your one-stop destination for premium textiles and quality oils for fashion and creative design.

With years of experience serving Aba's vibrant fashion community, we understand the needs of designers, tailors, and creative professionals. Our extensive collection includes the finest ankara, lace, george wrapper, senator materials, and specialized oils for fabric treatment and finishing.

We pride ourselves on competitive pricing, authentic materials, and personalized service that keeps our customers coming back. Whether you're a fashion designer working on a bridal collection or a tailor serving everyday clients, we have exactly what you need to bring your creative vision to life.
