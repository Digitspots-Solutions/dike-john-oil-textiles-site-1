About Us

Fountain Foods Specialty Co (also known as Fountain Foods Gourmet Tech Limited) is a leading FoodTech consultancy based in Aba North, Nigeria. Founded and led by experienced Food Safety Tech Consultants, we bridge the gap between traditional food production and modern safety standards. Our mission is to transform Nigeria's food industry by implementing cutting-edge safety protocols, quality assurance systems, and regulatory compliance frameworks. We work with restaurants, food manufacturers, processors, and distributors to build consumer trust through verified safety practices. With deep roots in Aba's vibrant commercial ecosystem, we understand the unique challenges facing Nigerian food businesses and provide practical, cost-effective solutions that drive both safety and profitability.

Our Process

1. Initial Consultation

We begin with a detailed discussion of your business, current practices, and safety goals. This no-obligation consultation helps us understand your unique needs.

2. Comprehensive Assessment

Our team conducts a thorough on-site evaluation of your facilities, processes, documentation, and staff practices against relevant standards.

3. Custom Action Plan

You receive a detailed report with specific, prioritized recommendations and a realistic timeline for implementation.

4. Implementation Support

We work alongside your team to execute improvements, from documentation systems to physical infrastructure upgrades.

5. Training & Certification

Your staff receives hands-on training in new procedures, ensuring everyone understands their role in maintaining safety standards.

6. Verification & Certification

We conduct final verification audits and assist with obtaining necessary certifications from regulatory bodies.

7. Continuous Improvement

Ongoing monitoring, periodic audits, and refresher training keep your systems current and effective.
